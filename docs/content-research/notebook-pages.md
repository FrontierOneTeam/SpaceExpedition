## Soil shelter
- title: Soil Shelter
- text: The more stuff between people and radiation, the safer they are. On the Moon, astronauts could pile soil, called regolith, over their shelters.
- source: `regolith-shield`
- quotes: "The more mass between the crew and radiation, the more likely that dangerous particles will deposit their energy before reaching the crew." / "On the Moon, astronauts could pile lunar soil, or regolith, over their shelters"
- glossary sentence available: "Regolith is the loose soil and broken rock on the ground of Mars and the Moon."
