# Spacekeepers credits

NASA observations are archived inputs. Production, damage, equipment incidents, warnings, the map and crew outcomes are game models.

## Sources actually shown

- [DONKI space weather records](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/): Real flare dates and classes in every chapter
- [NASA JPL news release, 29 September 2017: Large Solar Storm Sparks Global Aurora and Doubles Radiation Levels on the Martian Surface](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/large-solar-storm-sparks-global-aurora-and-doubles-radiation-levels-on-the-martian-surface/): The Mars radiation storm of 11 September 2017, and the MAVEN aurora
- [Curiosity rover radiation report, September 2017 (Southwest Research Institute)](https://www.swri.org/node/11852): The Mars radiation storm of 11 September 2017, and the MAVEN aurora
- [STCE space weather review, 4 to 15 September 2017](https://www.stce.be/esww15/program/Sept_2017_activity_summary.pdf): The 'biggest flare that solar cycle had seen' line on the 6 September card
- [NASA article, 10 June 2024: NASA Watches Mars Light Up During Epic Solar Storm](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/mars/nasa-watches-mars-light-up-during-epic-solar-storm/): The Mars radiation storm of 20 May 2024, and the MAVEN auroras
- [Perseverance Rover's SuperCam Records Wind on Mars, NASA Science, 10 March 2021](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/perseverance-rovers-supercam-records-wind-on-mars/): The sound of wind on Mars
- [NASA: Perseverance makes oxygen on Mars (MOXIE)](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-perseverance-mars-rover-extracts-first-oxygen-from-red-planet/): Sourced building and research explanations

- [NASA OSDR study OSD-745: lettuce grown on the space station](https://osdr.nasa.gov/osdr/data/osd/meta/745): Sourced building and research explanations
- [Canadian Space Agency: Growing healthy food in space and in remote areas](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/food-production/growing-healthy-food-in-space-and-remote-areas.asp): Sourced building and research explanations
- [NASA: Mapping buried water ice on Mars (SWIM)](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/mars/nasa-is-locating-ice-on-mars-with-this-new-map/): Sourced building and research explanations
- [NASA: How NASA will protect astronauts from space radiation at the Moon](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/heliophysics/how-nasa-will-protect-astronauts-from-space-radiation-at-the-moon/): Sourced building and research explanations
- [NASA: Construction technology for Moon and Mars exploration (MMPACT)](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/nasa-enables-construction-technology-for-moon-and-mars-exploration/): Sourced building and research explanations
